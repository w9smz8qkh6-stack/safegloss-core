# SafeGloss Core public roadmap

This roadmap contains reviewed, publicly disclosable, vendor-neutral
initiatives for SafeGloss Core. It is a filtered projection of the private
SafeGloss-wide strategy system; it does not expose Commercial, provider,
customer, research, analytics, billing, or private operational plans.

Roadmap horizons communicate sequence and confidence, not delivery-date
promises. An item appears here only after its complete public scope,
self-hosting implications, privacy/data boundary, and Core ownership are
reviewed. Public contributors may propose work through the repository's normal
issue and contribution process, but a proposal is not automatically a roadmap
commitment.

Promoted initiatives retain the `SG-####` identifier assigned by the private
strategy system. Run `python scripts/check_strategy_records.py` after editing
the roadmap; CI checks identifier uniqueness and table shape.

## Promotion and maintenance rules

- Core roadmap placement requires an outcome, audience, public scope,
  success/learning signal, important dependencies, and explicit maintainer
  approval.
- Shared work is defined in Core first and integrated into Commercial only
  after Core review and checks.
- Commercial-only ideas remain private and are never summarized here merely to
  make the roadmap appear complete.
- Current security, portability, documentation, test, and maintenance work
  remains part of the repository definition of done even when it is not listed
  as a feature initiative.
- Delivered items remain traceable through specifications, ADRs, changelog
  entries, commits, and releases after leaving the active horizon view.
- When a roadmap claim changes, update `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` if its durable
  active-work or integration summary also changed.
